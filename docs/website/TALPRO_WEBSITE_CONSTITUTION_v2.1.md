# Talpro Global Marketing Website Constitution v2.1

**Status:** Frozen and certified for implementation  
**Approval date:** 16 July 2026  
**Approved instruction:** Begin P0 restoration and continue sequentially through P1–P4. Do not launch paid marketing or issue the production certificate until 100% of mandatory controls pass with evidence.

## 1. Purpose and certification boundary

This constitution is the binding product, brand, content, technical, legal, conversion, candidate-experience, internationalisation, security, reliability and governance standard for `talproindia.com`.

- Constitution certification means the specification is complete enough to build.
- Production certification is separate and may be issued only after implementation evidence proves every mandatory gate has passed.
- “100% ready” means 100% of mandatory controls passed with evidence, no open Critical or High defects, and no unresolved legal, privacy or claim-verification blocker.
- The constitution must be reviewed quarterly and after major service, market, platform, regulatory or incident changes.

## 2. Master brand position

**Approved position:**

> Talpro is India’s Technology Talent and GCC Workforce Partner—helping global companies build, staff and scale high-performing technology teams in India.

**Master promise:**

> Talpro builds and scales India technology capability for global companies.

Talpro must not lead with “Enterprise IT Services” or software-development claims until it can demonstrate accountable engineering delivery leadership, methodology, commercial ownership and client evidence.

## 3. Offer truth

Approved offer families are:

1. GCC Advisory and Workforce Launch
2. Technology Talent Solutions
3. Contract Staffing and Staff Augmentation
4. Permanent Hiring and Executive Search
5. RPO and Managed Talent Capability
6. Managed Technology Delivery only after substantiation

A service may be published only when it has a named owner, process, commercial model, SLA, delivery capability, evidence, risk controls and clear inclusions/exclusions.

## 4. Audience journeys

- CHRO and Talent Acquisition leader
- Procurement and contingent-workforce leader
- Technology candidate

Every buyer page must answer why Talpro, why now, expected outcomes, operating method, evidence, risk control and next action.

## 6. Claims and evidence registry

Every public metric, testimonial, comparison, guarantee, compliance statement and commercial promise must record:

- source and owner
- calculation method and scope
- applicable period
- approval and expiry dates
- supporting evidence
- permitted public wording

Unverified claims must not ship. This applies particularly to `500+ placements`, `90%+ client retention`, `15+ years`, `48-hour shortlist`, `₹0 upfront`, `90-day guarantee`, healthcare/HIPAA statements, statutory-compliance claims and industry metrics.

## 7. Case studies and testimonials

A valid case study requires client context, problem, Talpro scope, timeline, actions, quantified outcome, calculation method and client authorisation or verified-anonymous approval.

- Composite or illustrative scenarios must not resemble verified testimonials.
- Employee-review proof must not be presented as client proof.
- Industry pages must not reuse irrelevant case studies or identical performance numbers.

## 8. Content and responsible AI

Content must be original, useful, accurate, expert-reviewed and owned by a named human.

- No scaled keyword-substitution industry pages.
- Research must disclose sources and methodology.
- AI may assist drafting, analysis and QA; a named accountable expert must approve publication.
- Every page has an owner, review date, expiry rule and archive decision.

## 9. Homepage standard

The homepage must contain:

1. outcome-oriented position
2. buyer-path selector
3. verified proof
4. principal solutions
5. delivery method
6. client outcomes
7. India/GCC intelligence
8. leadership accountability
9. compliance and enterprise trust
10. separate buyer and candidate conversion paths

Core positioning, CTAs, claims and legal identity must be server-rendered.

## 10. Conversion and revenue operations

Every business enquiry must pass tested consent/validation, CRM capture, account matching, qualification, routing, acknowledgement, SLA timing, escalation, opportunity feedback and attribution.

Mandatory controls include spam protection, delivery monitoring, UTM capture, deduplication, lead scoring, territory ownership, suppression, unsubscribe and test submissions after release.

The published response SLA must match operational capacity and be consistent across pages and automations.

## 11. Candidate and job experience

The website must provide real searchable vacancies, permanent job URLs, accurate details, expiry/removal, accessible applications, consent, acknowledgement, fraud protection, no-fee notices, data correction/deletion and valid JobPosting structured data.

“Roles publishing soon” is not sufficient for production certification.

## 12. International readiness

Global expansion requires region-specific buyer content, local proof, regional routing, time-zone handling, separate URLs for real regional/language variants, correct canonical/hreflang implementation and region-appropriate privacy controls.

## 13. Trust and procurement

The Trust and Procurement Centre must make it possible to verify legal identity, relevant registrations, privacy, information security, POSH, code of conduct, anti-bribery, workforce compliance, subprocessors/vendors, certifications/insurance where applicable, and DPA/SLA/vendor-onboarding processes.

Sensitive financial, tax or identity documents must not be published openly.

## 14. Privacy and consent

The implementation must support applicable India DPDP requirements, GDPR applicability, audience-specific notices, purpose-specific consent, cookie controls, retention/deletion, rights requests, processor inventory, international-transfer review and incident escalation. Qualified legal counsel must approve final notices and configurations.

## 15. Security, domain and email trust

Mandatory controls include HSTS, anti-clickjacking, MIME protection, restrictive permissions policy, hardened CSP without `unsafe-eval`, dependency/vulnerability scanning, MFA for administration, rate limiting, accessible bot protection, SPF, DKIM, DMARC, impersonation monitoring and incident response.

## 16. Reliability and disaster recovery

- Critical URLs must return their intended content across regions.
- CDN/load balancer and origin health must be monitored independently.
- `robots.txt`, sitemap, forms and conversion endpoints must be monitored.
- Backup restoration and rollback must be exercised.
- Major incidents require root-cause and prevention records.
- Production certification requires seven consecutive days without a Critical availability failure.

## 17. SEO and entity discoverability

Required controls include server-rendered core content, unique titles/descriptions/headings, canonical URLs, redirect map, XML sitemap, robots directives, appropriate structured data, original expert authorship, Search Console monitoring and crawl-error alerts.

Organization identity and official profile links must be consistent across the site and major public profiles.

## 18. Design, accessibility and performance

The website must use a documented design system, reusable accessible components, responsive layouts, keyboard operation, visible focus, labelled forms, sufficient contrast, captions/transcripts, reduced-motion support and tested device/browser coverage.

Production targets at the 75th percentile:

- LCP ≤ 2.5 seconds
- INP ≤ 200 milliseconds
- CLS ≤ 0.1
- WCAG 2.2 AA

## 19. Analytics and experimentation

Talpro must maintain a KPI dictionary, campaign taxonomy, consent-aware tagging, defined funnel stages, lead/opportunity/revenue attribution, content influence, candidate conversion and controlled experimentation rules.

Primary measures are qualified conversations, accepted opportunities, pipeline, revenue, delivery success and client retention—not vanity traffic.

## 20. Route integrity

Maintain an inventory of expected status, canonical URL, title, H1, template and owner for every published route. Every valid URL returns its intended page. Every invalid URL returns a genuine 404.

## 21. No soft 404s

A URL is broken if it returns HTTP 200 while displaying the homepage, a generic fallback or unrelated content. Monitoring must validate title, H1 and a content fingerprint—not status alone.

## 22. Server-rendered business truth

Positioning, service descriptions, primary CTAs, verified metrics and legal identity must remain accurate and useful without client-side JavaScript. Animated counters must render verified final values before enhancement.

## 23. Regulated claims

Healthcare, finance, government, security, privacy and statutory claims require qualified expert or legal approval. Talpro must not imply that individual candidate experience makes a client, project or system compliant.

## 24. Research publication

Salary guides and market reports require sample size, collection window, percentile definitions, minimum thresholds, role normalisation, outlier treatment, modelled-versus-observed disclosure, city methodology, publication date, review date and correction process.

## 25. Relevant legal architecture

Legal pages must reflect Talpro’s actual business. Irrelevant template policies must be removed from primary navigation unless genuinely applicable. Required pages include privacy/data rights, website/client terms, candidate fee/fraud policy, grievance, procurement/compliance, accessibility and responsible disclosure as applicable.

## 26. Release evidence pack

Every release must produce:

- route and soft-404 report
- broken-link and redirect report
- form-to-CRM evidence
- structured-data validation
- accessibility results
- Core Web Vitals/performance results
- security scan
- claim-registry approvals
- privacy/legal approvals
- backup/rollback confirmation

## Implementation phases

### P0 — Restoration and route integrity

- Stabilise origin/CDN and eliminate intermittent 5xx failures.
- Restore Case Studies, Privacy, Compliance, Grievance, About and service routes.
- Implement genuine 404 handling and content-fingerprint monitoring.
- Restore and validate `robots.txt` and sitemap.
- Remove duplicated homepage content.
- Replace broken animated counters with server-rendered values.
- Correct stale software-development metadata.
- Validate all navigation/footer links and security headers.

### P1 — Positioning and offer architecture

- Apply the approved brand position.
- Separate GCC, staffing, permanent hiring, executive search and RPO offers.
- Hide unsupported IT-delivery and industry claims.
- Implement the claim registry.
- Reconcile SLA and salary-guide discrepancies.

### P2 — Trust, candidates and conversion

- Publish verified case studies and accountable leadership.
- Build the Trust and Procurement Centre.
- Launch real job architecture and candidate protections.
- Connect and test forms through CRM, ownership, SLA and attribution.

### P3 — Global authority

- Build GCC and India talent intelligence.
- Add buyer-specific and regional journeys.
- Publish expert-led, original research and thought leadership.

### P4 — Production certification

Production certification requires all mandatory gates to pass, no Critical/High defects, claim/legal approval, end-to-end conversion proof, accessibility/security/performance compliance, exercised restore/rollback, and seven-day production stability.
