# P2 Case Study Evidence Gate

No case study is approved for publication at this checkpoint.

Each future case study must include all of the following before its route,
navigation entry, sitemap URL, structured data, or promotional excerpt is enabled:

1. Client context and whether the client may be named.
2. Defined problem, scope, timeline, and Talpro actions.
3. Quantified outcome with source system, period, numerator, denominator, and
   calculation method.
4. Delivery-owner approval of the operational facts.
5. Finance approval where the outcome includes commercial or cost information.
6. Client authorization or an approved verified-anonymous release.
7. Legal and marketing approval of the final wording.
8. Review and expiry dates with an accountable owner.

Until these items exist, `/case-studies` remains redirected away from the public
site and the claim registry keeps case-study outcomes blocked.
