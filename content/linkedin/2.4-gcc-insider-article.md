## Title: Why 40% of New GCCs in India Fail at Hiring — And How to Fix It

### The Promise vs The Reality

India now hosts 1,600+ Global Capability Centers. By 2030, that number is expected to cross 2,400.

Every multinational setting up a GCC hears the same pitch: "India has 5 million IT professionals. Talent is abundant."

Then reality hits.

### The 3 Hiring Traps New GCCs Fall Into

**Trap 1: The Salary Benchmarking Gap**

Most GCCs benchmark salaries against their home country and apply a discount. "If we pay 60% of US rates, we'll be competitive."

Wrong. You're not competing with US salaries. You're competing with Flipkart, Razorpay, PhonePe, and 200 other well-funded Indian companies — plus 1,599 other GCCs.

A senior DevOps engineer in Bangalore commands 22-38 LPA today. That's up 10% from last year. If your budget is stuck at 2023 rates, your offer letters will keep getting rejected.

**Trap 2: The "Post and Pray" Approach**

New GCCs often start with a small HR team and job postings on Naukri. This works for generic roles.

For specialized positions — Platform Engineers, MLOps Engineers, Rust Developers — the best candidates aren't on job boards. They're passively employed, getting 3-4 recruiter messages daily, and only respond to personalized outreach that demonstrates technical understanding.

**Trap 3: The Speed Problem**

Indian tech talent moves FAST. The best candidates receive offers within 7-10 days of starting their search. GCCs with 4-round interview processes spanning 3-4 weeks lose 60% of their shortlisted candidates to faster-moving companies.

### The Playbook That Works

GCCs that nail hiring in India share three patterns:

1. **They hire a strong India talent leader in Month 1** — not Month 6
2. **They use specialist staffing partners** who understand their specific tech stack — not generalist agencies who send 50 resumes hoping 2 stick
3. **They move at Indian market speed** — first interview within 48 hours of receiving a profile, offer within 2 weeks

### The Data

From our placement data across 14 years of serving GCCs in India:

| Metric | Average GCC | Top-Performing GCC |
|--------|------------|-------------------|
| Time to first shortlist | 3-4 weeks | 48 hours |
| Offer acceptance rate | 55% | 82% |
| 90-day retention | 78% | 94% |
| Interview rounds | 5-6 | 3 |

The difference isn't luck. It's process.

### What I'd Tell Every New GCC Leader

You didn't set up a center in India to struggle with hiring. The talent exists. The market is competitive but navigable.

The companies that win are the ones that treat Indian hiring as a specialized discipline — not an extension of their global HR playbook.
