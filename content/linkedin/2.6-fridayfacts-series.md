## Edition 2: The Speed Tax

#TalProFridayFacts

The average time to fill a senior engineering role in Bangalore: 42 days.

The best-in-class? 18 days.

That 24-day gap costs companies approximately:
→ ₹3-5 lakh in lost productivity per vacant seat
→ 60% candidate dropout (they take other offers)
→ Recruiter fatigue (team starts lowering the bar)

The #1 predictor of fast hiring isn't salary budget.

It's time-to-first-interview.

Companies that conduct the first technical round within 48 hours of receiving a profile fill roles 2.3x faster than those who take 7+ days.

Speed IS your competitive advantage.

## Edition 3: The GCC Salary Surprise

#TalProFridayFacts

There are now 1,600+ GCCs operating in India.

Here's what most people don't realize about GCC salaries:

GCCs pay 15-25% MORE than Indian product companies for the same role.

But their offer acceptance rate is LOWER.

Why? Three reasons:
1. Slower hiring process (avg 6 rounds vs 3)
2. Less exciting tech narrative ("you'll maintain our global platform" vs "you'll build from scratch")
3. Brand recognition gap (candidates know Razorpay, not your parent company's India arm)

The fix isn't more money. It's better storytelling and faster movement.

The GCCs winning talent are the ones who sell the mission, not the multinational brand.

## Edition 4: The Python-to-AI Pipeline

#TalProFridayFacts

5 years ago, Python developers were hired at 5-10 LPA (junior) for web backends.

Today, the same Python skills are the entry point for:
→ AI/ML Engineering (8-15 LPA junior, 45-75 LPA lead)
→ Data Engineering (6-12 LPA junior, 36-58 LPA lead)
→ MLOps (8-14 LPA junior, 40-62 LPA lead)

Python didn't become more valuable. The ECOSYSTEM around it did.

What this means for hiring:
1. Your "Python developer" job posting is competing with AI companies
2. Django/Flask developers are pivoting to LangChain and FastAPI
3. Pure backend Python roles are harder to fill because talent is moving upstream

If you're still hiring Python devs at 2022 budgets — you're fishing in an empty pond.

The talent moved. Your offers need to move too.
